# 1. Defining a Product Metric

Question Type: “How would you measure success of [product/feature]?”

# 3-Step Framework:

# Step 1: Clarify the Product & Its Purpose

Why is the company building this?

What’s the product goal?

Who is it for?

How does it work?

# Step 2: Explain Product & Business Goals

Tie the Product goal (what makes users happy) to the Business goals (inturn what makes the company grow)

# Step 3: Define Success Metrics

Follow the AARRR funnel and align each metric with product/business goals:

Acquisition — how do users discover or start using the feature?

Activation — what indicates they’ve tried and successfully used it once?

Engagement — how do we know they’re using it regularly?

Retention — how do we know they’re coming back to use it again?

Revenue — how does this feature contribute to business goals?
